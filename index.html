Self-Test: The 4 Ears Model

Based on the Communication Model by Friedemann Schulz von Thun

Instructions: According to Schulz von Thun, every message has four layers. We often have a "favorite ear" with which we prefer to listen. This test helps you identify your dominant listening habit.

Please choose the reaction that comes most naturally to you. Be honest and spontaneous!

1. Cashier Situation: You've been waiting long. The cashier groans: "It's so stressful today."
2. Breakfast: Partner asks "Are there any rolls left?"
3. Cycling: Friend says "I want to ride a new big loop today."
4. Message: "I'm coming home later today."
5. Reading Harry Potter: Someone asks "What exciting thing are you reading?"
6. Missing Item: "I can't find the shopping list."
7. Traffic: Pedestrian yells "Where did you learn to drive?"
8. Stranger: "Do you have a watch/the time?"
9. Boss: "Where are the documents?"
10. Upset: Friend says "Just relax."
11. Colleague: "It took you that long for this?"
12. Elderly: Children ask "How long do you want to live here alone?"
13. Colleague groans: "There is still so much to do!"
14. Leaf Blower noise outside.

How the evaluation works

This test counts which level you chose for each scenario. There are 14 questions in total.

  • Factual Ear (Blue): You prefer to focus on the objective content. You stick to the facts and often miss emotional undertones.
  • Relationship Ear (Red): You take things personally. You often feel criticized, attacked, or assume the statement says something about your standing with the other person.
  • Self-Revelation Ear (Yellow): You focus on the sender. You ask: "How is he feeling?" or "Why is he saying this?". This is the ear of empathy (or sometimes diagnostics).
  • Appeal Ear (Green): You have an "action bias". You hear a command or a request in everything. You want to please, help, or fix the situation immediately.
